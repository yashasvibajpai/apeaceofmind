# 📻 San Andreas Radio: A Peace of Mind 🧠 😌

*Ah shit, here we go again... but this time, it's just vibes.*

Need your peace but everything is so noisy and hotch-potch around you? Welcome to **San Andreas Radio**, your ultimate background noise generator straight out of 1992 Los Santos!

Whether you're cruising down Grove Street, flying a Dodo over Mount Chiliad, or just trying to get some work done in the real world, this repo has got you covered with the iconic sounds of GTA San Andreas.

## 🎮 What's in the trunk?

This repository contains a minimal, frontend-only web application that serves as a time machine to your favorite in-game radio stations. No bloated frameworks, no 500MB `node_modules` folder—just good ol' **Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

## 🚀 Features (or "Functionality" if you're a cop)

- **Authentic Streams**: Sourcing MP3 audio streams directly from the Internet Archive. It's like finding a mixtape in the back of a stolen Tahoma.
- **Classic Stations**: All your favorites are here! From the funky beats of *Bounce FM* to the country twang of *K-Rose*, and the smooth reggae of *K-Jah West*.
- **Random Seek Technology**: To simulate the real radio experience, whenever you tune in, it randomly seeks to a spot in the track. You never know what you're gonna get!
- **Continuous Loop**: The audio loops automatically when it ends, ensuring the vibe never dies.
- **Pricedown Font**: We even styled it with the classic GTA font so your eyes can feel the nostalgia too.

## 🛠️ How to tune in?

Just open up `index.html` in your favorite web browser and click on a station. It's that simple! No need to enter any cheat codes (though `HESOYAM` is always appreciated).
